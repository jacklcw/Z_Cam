- New: Zed, a little pixel pet that lives on the center screen. It crawls the screen edges and ceiling, hops onto and hangs under your floating windows, plays with a ball and bugs, and eats, showers, uses the toilet and does sport on its own.
- Zed follows the time of day (calm at night, lively by day), reacts to fast driving, gets tired and suffers when the car battery is low, and looks very satisfied while charging.
- Tap Zed for a small reaction, press and hold to tickle it (it pushes the finger away, struggles or relaxes), drag it anywhere.
- Zed settings card in the app: on/off, colour or rainbow, face (Visor, Lenses, Screen), size, pace, and toggles for hopping, crawling, toys, naps, daily life and reactions. It can also be hidden from the floating ring.
