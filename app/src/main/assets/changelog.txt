0.79.0
- New Trip widget (settings: "Trip widget" card, gauge icon). A floating window like the cameras: drag it anywhere, resize to any shape with the edge holders, the corner grip or a pinch, hide it into the top dock (speedometer chip), hide it with the floating button, and toggle it from the floating ring (Floating windows group).
- Shows speed, throttle / brake / regen pills (red / light red / green), gear, drive mode, HV battery %, a 60 s chart and trip tiles. Glance shows 4 tiles you pick from 14 metrics (Wh/km, average, distance, range, top speed, regen share, HV and 12V battery, 0-100, peak accel, peak brake, 100-0, energy used, stopped time); Full shows them all. Tiles draw a small chart along their bottom while their value changes.
- Chart lines: load, speed, G force and battery, any combination; braking turns the line red.
- Look: vertical, horizontal or auto layout, font (Inter, JetBrains Mono, Orbitron, Condensed, Rajdhani), theme, accent and background colour with the app colour picker, edge fade, opacity, km or miles, battery size for Wh/km. Header height matches the other windows. Needs the relay updated (ZDash 0.98.0) for battery, range and 12V.
- Camera windows after a drive: the car moving now counts as "not parked" straight away, and a 15 minute watchdog restarts the service if the system killed it or refused the start at boot, so you no longer have to open the app once.
- Data usage: the app counts what it sends and receives (new Data usage button with a daily graph, also uploaded to the dashboard). The on-device log now keeps 3 days.
- Live view saves mobile data: no capture while nobody watches, a stream ends after 60 s without viewers or at 6 min / 120 MB, and a failing queued snapshot upload backs off (up to 30 min) instead of retrying on every poll.

0.77.0
- Civic Type R engine sound retuned from real FL5 recordings: fuller idle, darker revs at high rpm, no turbo whistle, no hiss.
- Key-on like the real car: short crank, a louder burst, then a fast idle (~1500 rpm) that holds for a few seconds. The Civic now actually cranks.
- Faster pedal response (the engine reacts almost instantly; the car's throttle read is also lighter).
- Smoother gear shifts: the sound no longer drops out while the rpm falls, and the shift bang is a soft thump instead of a crack. No more pops and crackle when you lift off.
- Play welcome sound now plays the whole start-up (it used to be cut after a few seconds).
- Web HUD: the floating window has its own switch; turning the HUD on brings it along, and hiding the HUD page leaves it up.
- Removed the Camping change diagnostic button.
