0.101.0
- Pin button in every floating window header (cameras, web, trip): a pinned window stays when you minimize everything with the floating button.
- The floating button now shows whenever its switch is on, even with no floating window turned on, and starts at boot.

0.100.0
- Floating arc: hover an acceleration mode to set its steering (Light, Standard, Heavy) and regeneration (Standard, Strong) right on the arc. Tap the chosen option again to leave the car as is.

0.99.4
- Speed limit: the glow and beep only trigger once you are a whole km/h over the limit, so driving at 60 in a 60 zone no longer alerts.

0.99.3
- Credits: now shows the GPL-3.0 license notice, no warranty, the Zeekr Shortcut code credit and the source link; the engine sound is credited as dts88's original work.
- Log: keeps more lines (4000) for relay upload, and the speed debug log adds a 10 s heartbeat away from the limit.

0.99.2
- Speed limit: new debug switch that logs your driving speed (car sensor or GPS) near the limit to the log, so the glow can be compared with the real speed.

0.99.1
- Pairing: new Change relay button on the Cameras and pairing code screens, so you can point the car at a different relay (clears the relay and link and shows the setup QR again).

0.99.0
- Zed no longer walks onto the system status bar (his area now stops at the bars), so you cannot tap him there.
- Reports whether a front seat is occupied and whether the gear is in P to the relay (used by ZDash Auto-lock).
- Shows the Camping reminder and Auto-lock on hold alerts in the car notification pane (needs ZDash with a relay).

0.98.0
- Speed limit sign: new sliders to adjust the red border thickness and the number size (included in settings backup).

0.97.0
- Zed has a natural body-shaped shadow (soft drop shadow plus a thin dark contour) so he stays visible on any background.
- Zed now spends little time on the right 30% of the screen, where the steering wheel hides it: he mostly walks, hops, hangs and climbs on the left.
- Zed is round when rested and gets slimmer the more you drive today (60 km is fully fit). At midnight he smoothly returns to his round body.
